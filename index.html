About Northstar

Built for businesses that are done guessing what's working.

Northstar Growth Studio is a growth, web and automation studio based in Slough, Berkshire, working with small and mid-sized businesses across the UK. We plan the strategy, build the website or system, and set up the automation that carries the workload — so enquiries keep arriving whether or not anyone's watching the inbox.

We started Northstar because too many good businesses were being let down by agencies that treated a website as the finish line rather than the starting point. Everything we build is judged on one thing: does it produce more qualified enquiries, bookings or sales than before.

Clients choose us because we stay close to the numbers, explain decisions in plain English, and treat every project like it's our own business on the line — not because we're the biggest name in the room, but because we're the most accountable one.
